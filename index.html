Race: thirteen gates down an open mountain. Follow the arrow and the light beam to the next gate and pick your own line between them. Freeride: go anywhere and hit kickers, booters, rock ledges and ridges for style points. Skinnies: a balance course of narrow raised planks; ride slow, correct the wobble, don't dab. Drag to steer. Hold Hop through dips to pump and let go on a lip to pop. In the air, Brake lifts the nose and Hop drops it. Tilt control makes the phone your handlebar: roll it to steer, tip it away to tuck and pump (nose down in the air), tip it toward you to brake (nose up in the air). Keyboard: ←→ steer, ↓ brake, Space hop, V view.
